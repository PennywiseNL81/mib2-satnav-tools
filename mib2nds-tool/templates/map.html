MIB2 Map checker

Check a downloaded map (folder with maps/, .zip or .7z): compatibility with your profile, instant country coverage, search + coverage map after a one-time conversion. Plus update-check, SD-updater and cleanup.

8Update SD card (cardreader)

    
Choose a local package from downloads/. The card is
       first fully backed up to BACKUP/SDcard_<date>/, then the
       existing maps/ are replaced and, if your profile says so
       (car.workaround), maps/EEC/EEC_WLD/OVERALL.NDS
       is restored to the original from the original release.
